Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

✓ Budget Toolkit does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What data does the app store?

Budget Toolkit stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Income and expenditure figures you enter
  • Monthly savings amounts and targets
  • Your savings goal label and target amount

This data is written to a single file (budget-data.json) stored in your device's private app data directory. It is never uploaded, synced to a server, or transmitted anywhere.

Does the app use the internet?

Budget Toolkit makes no network requests. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no third-party trackers of any kind included in the app.

Does the app access any device features?

The app requests access to device storage solely to read and write your budget data file. No other device features (camera, contacts, location, microphone) are used or requested.

Third-party links

The Providers page contains links to third-party lender websites. These open in your device's browser. Budget Toolkit has no control over those websites and their own privacy policies apply once you leave the app.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be available at this URL and within the app. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
